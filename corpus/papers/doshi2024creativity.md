# Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content

## 1. Executive Summary
Does generative AI diversify or standardize human culture? Doshi and Hauser (*Science Advances*, 2024) conducted a randomized trial with human writers tasked with composing short creative stories under varying degrees of generative AI assistance (ranging from unassisted human drafting to receiving 1 or 5 AI-generated story ideas).

The findings establish the empirical reality of the **Social Dilemma of Generative AI**:
- **Individual Gain**: AI assistance boosts the evaluated quality, engagement, and perceived creativity of individual writers—providing the greatest uplift to writers who were initially evaluated as less creative.
- **Collective Homogenization**: However, at the population level, stories written with AI assistance were **substantially more similar to each other** in semantic embedding space and narrative structure than unassisted stories. 

By anchoring multiple human creators to the same foundation model attractor, AI induces an aggregate loss of creative diversity across the cultural collective.

---

## 2. Second-Order Cybernetic Framing

### 2.1 The Collective Action Trap
In second-order cybernetics, individual micro-decisions aggregate into macro-systemic pathologies:
- Each individual writer has a rational incentive to use generative AI because it raises their personal quality score and lowers cognitive friction.
- However, as millions of independent agents simultaneously optimize their personal output against the same central model, the collective variety of the entire communicative ecosystem contracts.
- Over time, the cultural substrate is drained of unpredictable exploratory mutations, starving the evolutionary system of novelty.

---

## 3. Empirical Methodology & Data Substrates

- **Participants**: 293 professional and amateur writers composing original fiction.
- **Evaluators**: 600 independent human judges performing 4,395 blind evaluations of story quality, emotional resonance, and novelty.
- **Computational Measurement**: Dense sentence transformer embeddings (RoBERTa and Sentence-BERT) measuring cosine similarity across all pairs of generated stories within each experimental condition.

---

## 4. Key Findings & Quantitative Indicators

1. **Individual Quality Boost**: Access to generative AI increased the evaluated quality of stories by **up to 9%**, with the largest gains observed among writers with lower baseline creativity scores.
2. **Collective Diversity Contraction**: Pairwise similarity between stories written with AI assistance was significantly higher ($p < 0.001$) than among stories written without AI, proving that AI compresses the semantic volume of human creative output.
3. **The Homogenization Trade-off**: The individual incentive to consume AI output directly accelerates population-wide cultural conformity.
